Moon and Mars
NASA's long-term archive for planetary science data from NASA orbital, lander, and rover missions. It provides access to lunar and Martian mission observations spanning imagery, geoscience, atmospheres, plasma environments, cartography, and other measurements. Link: https://pds.nasa.gov/?utm_source=%27chatgpt.com%27
LRO provides extensive observations of the lunar surface through instruments including LROC, LOLA, Diviner, LEND, Mini-RF and others. link: https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/lro/?utm_source=%27chatgpt.com%27
Diviner maps lunar surface temperatures and provides especially useful products for studying the lunar poles. Its polar and resource products could be compared with extreme cold, ice-bearing, or other terrestrial environments. Link: https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/lro/diviner.htm?utm_source=%27chatgpt.com%27

Space Agency Partner Resources

RCM is Canada's new generation of Earth observation satellites. The RCM includes a trio of Earth observation satellites, capable of scanning Earth day or night and in any weather conditions. The RCM is designed to provide effective solutions in three main areas: Maritime surveillance (ice, surface wind, oil pollution and ship monitoring); Disaster management (mitigation, warning, response and recovery); and Ecosystem monitoring (agriculture, wetlands, forestry and coastal change monitoring). Link: https://donnees-data.asc-csa.gc.ca/en/dataset/rcm
Launched in November 1995, RADARSAT-1 provided Canada and the world with an operational radar satellite system capable of timely delivery of large amounts of data. Equipped with a powerful synthetic aperture radar (SAR) instrument, it acquired images of the Earth day or night, in all weather and through cloud cover, smoke and haze. All data products in the Canadian RADARSAT-1 archive have been released under an open license. Link: https://donnees-data.asc-csa.gc.ca/en/dataset/radarsat1
This tutorial demonstrates how to work with SAR imagery using Python and QGIS. It includes easy and practical steps for accessing and visualizing data link: https://github.com/asc-csa/Synthetic_Aperture_Radar_Tutorial
